*Ongoing remote research collaboration with Prof. Özgür Gürbüz — Oct. 2026 – Present*

* Modeling a 3D THz aerial routing environment as a finite-horizon Markov Decision Process (MDP) accounting for probabilistic rain cells, frequency-dependent pointing jitter, and relay queue contention.
* Designing a Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) agent alongside an analytical routing heuristic to jointly optimize multi-hop relay selection and sub-band allocation, balancing traffic and mitigating link outages.

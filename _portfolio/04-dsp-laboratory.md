* Implemented an adaptive Least Mean Squares (LMS) noise canceller for unknown FIR channels, analyzing convergence stability, steady-state error, and filter-order trade-offs.
* Engineered an FFT-based Overlap-Save block convolution pipeline with multirate decimation and coherent pilot-tone demodulation for raw wideband I/Q captures.
* Built a phase-invariant matched-filter detector and evaluated detection performance via Monte Carlo simulations under additive white Gaussian noise.

To overcome the inability of standard regression models to generalize across broadband Terahertz frequencies, multiple atmospheric profiles, and diverse aerial link geometries, this work introduces two physics-informed neural architectures:

* **Physics-Anchored Residual Network (PARN):** Predicts broadband THz atmospheric absorption using a single unified model anchored to physical propagation priors.
* **Physics-Anchored Mixture-of-Experts (PA-MoE):** Uses regional gating and specialized experts to simultaneously capture smooth transmission windows and sharp molecular resonance peaks with high accuracy.

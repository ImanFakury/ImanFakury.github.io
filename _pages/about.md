Hi! I am **Iman Alizadeh Fakouri**, a B.S. graduate in Electrical Engineering from **Sharif University of Technology** (GPA: 18.76/20, Top 10%). Currently, I am collaborating remotely with Prof. Özgür Gürbüz at Sabancı University on machine learning and reinforcement learning methods for Terahertz (THz) aerial wireless networks.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate thesis under Prof. Babak Khalaj on LLM-driven fault diagnosis for cloud-native 5G networks, and spent Summer 2026 as a PURE research intern at Sabancı University in Istanbul. My work sits at the intersection of wireless communications, physical-layer modeling, and machine learning. Feel free to [reach out](mailto:imanfakury1382@gmail.com) if you would like to collaborate or chat.

## Research Interests
* Next-generation wireless communications (5G/6G & Terahertz aerial networks)
* Physics-informed machine learning and channel modeling
* Reinforcement learning for dynamic routing and resource allocation
* LLM-assisted network automation and cloud-native mobile systems
* Signal processing, estimation theory, and generative modeling
